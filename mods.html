GX-LAUNCHER // MODS
Autoclicker radmanplays this mod clicks for you
AutoFish ZXMushroom63 fishes for you. just throw a fishing rod in water to use this mod
AutoRespawn Oeil-de-Lynx respawns you automatically when you die
Blink ZXMushroom63 use by typing the .blinkon and .blinkoff commands and sending it
Blur Murturtle drops the framerate to 5 fps while the tab is unfocused and restores it when you come back
ChatCommands Neverflagastralis adds dot commands like .help, .pos, .time, .spawn, .lastpos, .goto and .setpos, plus a song player (.play, .pause, .volume, .loop)
Chat Shortcuts ZXMushroom63 shortcuts: {health}, {pos}, {name}, {me}, {x}, {y}, {z}, {level}, {walked}, {chunk}
Duck ZXMushroom63 adds ducks to the game
FasterMinecarts ZXMushroom63 faster minecarts! use /minecart_speed {factor} to change the speed. carts are very prone to being flung off rails
FreshEaglerUI Murturtle reshuffles the main menu buttons and slides inventory screens in with an animation
Fullbright radmanplays full bright mod for eaglerforge. keybind(enabled by default): f
GrappleHook ZXMushroom63 grappling hook mod. how to use: 1.grab a fishing rod 2.use the fishing rod 3.grappling hook!
Jetpack radmanplays allows you to fly as if you had a jetpack. keybind(hold): h
JumpBoost Oeil-de-Lynx lets you keep jumping in mid-air by pressing space, up to 3 boosts per jump
Music BarneyTheGod Plays a users music through a direct mp3 link. Saves music through Local Browser storage.
NoFall ZXMushroom63 makes you not take fall damage
NoParticles Unknown turns off all particles, which can help performance
PlayerVision Oeil-de-Lynx pick a player from a dropdown and see the game through their eyes
Recipe Oeil-de-Lynx This is a recipe mod for eaglerforge, a bit like JEI.
SemiAutoLogin Murturtle asks for your password in a popup, then press the up arrow key to send /login with it
Speed radmanplays makes you faster
StatsHud Oeil-de-Lynx stats hud overlay (the code is obfuscated, so this is going by the name)
VeinMiner ZXMushroom63 vein-mines trees & ores while crouching. press the 'config' button for settings
WayPoint blizz828, Block_2222 & ZXMushroom63 use /setwp <name> to make a waypoint, and /wp <name> to go to it. /remwp <name> to delete a waypoint. /listwp to list all waypoints
WorldEdit radmanplays use //wand, //set and //walls in singleplayer worlds
Xray radmanplays simple xray mod. its recommended to use this with Fullbright. keybind: x
